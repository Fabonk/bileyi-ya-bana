Cantique n° 402

Mapendezi ya dunia

1Mapendezi ya dunia
sitamani tena,
Nina-acha yote Yesu,
nifanane nawe.

RefrainNifanane nawe Yesu,
ao nyumbani ao popote;
Kila siku ya maisha yangu,
Nifanane nawe,

2Ulivunja kila pingu
la makosa yangu,
Hata nikutumikie,
nifanane nawe.

3Toka hapa kwenda mbinguni
kwa safari nzima.
Nitapasha Neno lako,
nifanane nawe

4Nikukute kule mbingu,
Mukombozi wangu,
Nisikie neno “ vema”,
nifanane nawe
