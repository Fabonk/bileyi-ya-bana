Cantique n° 781

Zimpasi zingi

1Zimpasi zingi vava nsi,
Mayela tueti monanga,
Mansangaza madadanga,
O wiza kutusadisa

RefrainO Mpeve wiza, o wiza
O wiza kutusadisa.

2Ye nsatu a biabio
Bianza bu yet kutufikita
Ye mpukumunu zizidi
O wiza kutusadisa

3Zimvita zingi vava nsi
Malema tueti monanga
Bidimbu bieti monika,
O wiza kutusadisa.

4Zimpangi zami vava nsi
Bafioti ye mindele mpe
Tutatamana mu sambila
Neteye kuna nsukulu
