Cantique n° 443

Mumanyi munene wa mvita

1Mumanyi munene wa mvita,
Mutshimunyi wa kale kale,
Tshilobo tshikole mu mvita
Yesu ukena kutshimuna

RefrainTshilobo
Djuka wenda
Madiunda mubaluishi bebe
Bana bebe babandila
Luendu luebe lua butshimunyi

2Misumba yonso ya mu diulu
Idi panyima pebe wewe,
Bwa kutumbisha dina diebe,
Mumanyi munene wa mvita

3Diboko diebe dia bukola
Didi dibandishibwe kulu
Pamutu pa makola anso
Nena manya onso a mvita

4Dituku dikadi pa buipi,
Diwa kulula bantu babi
Ne mwambi wabo wa mashini
Popamue ne nyama wa lonyi
