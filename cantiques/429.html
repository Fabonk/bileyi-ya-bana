Cantique n° 429

Mon espoir ne se fonde “ditekemena dinene”

1Ditekemena dinene,
Anu ku mashi à Yesu
Tshiena ngeyemena munga
Anu Yesu Musungidi

RefrainYesu ntshieyemenu tshianyi,
Ndi ngeyemena kudiye (2X)

2Palua midima mikole,
Yesu ntshieyemenu tshianyi
Palua tshipupu tshikole,
Yesu undi ngeyemena

3Mashi andi akamata,
Adi tshipungidi tshiandi
Palua butekete kundi
Nyeye udi unkolesha.

4Paluaye kunshikidilu,
Ndi musue kuikala nende
Mu disanka dia kashidi
Kumpala kua nkuasa wende.
