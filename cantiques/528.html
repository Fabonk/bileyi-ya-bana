Cantique n° 528

Ô Yesu wami mvulusi

1O Yesu wami Mvulusi, ibanzanga na de,
Zinzungu ziwandungulua
Mu Ngetesemane

RefrainKilendi vilakana ko, kingongo
Kiaku ye, zinzungu ziwandungulua,
Mu Ngetesemane

2Wafukama wasambila, mu wonga waku Ye
Watatamana yuvula,
Luzolo lua Dise.

3Ye mu minkanu miami Se
Wamona zitu mpe,
Wavibidila Nkwamusu, mu zola kwa nade

4Vo ntima mi yikidi, nazizia mpe kua nge,
Usonga bu wandungulua
Mu Ngetesemane.
